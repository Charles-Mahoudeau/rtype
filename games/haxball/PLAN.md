# Haxball: plan

A demo game for the AYLO Engine with the concept of haxball: a top-down football match, up to 4 against 4, where
players are round, push the ball with their body and kick it with one button. Written in Luau only (no C++), in
`games/haxball/`, laid out like `games/gyatt_rider_2/`. It shows off the **physics** (every collision and bounce),
**particles** (kicks, ball trail, goals, dust), **networking** (authoritative server, inputs, replicated state, custom
packets) and the **UI** (title menu, team picker, HUD).

Decisions taken with the user (2026-11-08): up to 4v4 with a team picker; sprite art from a free CC0 pack; a small UI
title menu before the match; this plan lives in the repository. The packs could not be downloaded without a browser,
so the art and the sounds are generated by the scripts in `tools/` instead (section 5).

## 1. The game

**Rules.** Two teams, red and blue, up to 4 players each; everyone else spectates. A match lasts 3 minutes or ends at the
first team to 5 goals. A draw when time runs out goes to golden goal (the next goal wins). After a goal everyone goes
back to their half and the team that conceded kicks off after a 3-2-1 countdown, during which nobody moves. After the
end, the winner is shown for 6 seconds and a new match starts with the same teams.

**Controls.** WASD or the arrows to move, Space or X to kick (held: the player's outline goes white and the next touch
within reach kicks), Escape to open the team picker again.

**Feel (haxball's model).** A player accelerates toward the input and is slowed by damping, which caps its speed.
Holding kick makes it slower. A kick is an impulse on the ball, away from the player, when the ball is within reach.
The ball is lighter than players, bounces off the walls and posts, and slows down by itself.

**Cut on purpose** (to stay quick): haxball's kickoff rule that keeps players out of the opponent's half, chat, admin
commands, several stadiums, replays, bots.

## 2. Screens

1. **Title menu** (UI plugin): the game's name, a name field, three buttons *Red*, *Blue* and *Spectate*, each with how
   many players the team has (*Red 2/4*). A full team's button is disabled. Clicking sends `game.Join` to the server.
2. **Match**: the pitch fills the window. HUD at the top: red score, timer, blue score. Every player's name sits under
   them and the local player's sprite has a marker. Escape reopens the picker over the match (switching teams sends
   `game.Join` again).
3. **Banners**: "3, 2, 1, GO" at kickoff, "GOAL! <name>" for 2 seconds, "OVERTIME", "<RED|BLUE> WINS" at the end.

## 3. Data

All sizes are in world units = pixels of a 1280x720 virtual view. The constants live in `shared/rules.luau`: pitch
1100x520, goal 170 wide and 40 deep, player radius 18, ball radius 11, speeds, damping, kick strength.

### Replicated components (`shared/rules.luau`)

| Component | Fields | Replication |
|---|---|---|
| `game.Player` | `team` (u8: 0 spectator, 1 red, 2 blue), `name` (string), `position`, `velocity` (vec2), `kicking` (bool) | everyChange |
| `game.Ball` | `position`, `velocity` (vec2) | everyChange |
| `game.Match` | `state` (u8: waiting, kickoff, playing, scored, ended), `timeLeft`, `countdown` (f32), `red`, `blue` (u8), `overtime` (bool), `winner` (u8) | everyChange |
| `game.Fixture` | `position` (vec2), on each wall and post, so a client places its collider (F3) | spawnOnly |

The client draws the pitch as one generated sprite, from the same constants.

### Server-only components

`game.Kicker` (`armed` bool, `cooldown` f32) on each player. The match's own clock lives in `game.Match`.

### Custom packets (`engine.net.packet`, `shared/packets.luau`)

| Packet | Direction | Fields | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `game.Join` | toServer, reliable | `team` u8, `name` string 16 | the title menu and the team picker |
| `game.Kicked` | toClients, unreliable | `x`, `y`, `power` f32 | particles and sound where the kick happened |
| `game.Bounce` | toClients, unreliable | `x`, `y`, `speed` f32 | the ball hit a post or a wall: sparks, thud |
| `game.Goal` | toClients, reliable | `team` u8, `scorer` string 16, `ownGoal` bool, `x`, `y` f32 | confetti, cheer, banner |

The clients cannot see collisions (only the server steps the physics), so the server sends these one-shot packets.

### Inputs (`shared/rules.luau`)

`move` (vector2: WASD, arrows) and `kick` (button: Space, X), read on the server with `rtype.replication.readInput`.

### Physics

| Body | Shape | Body | Layers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ball | circle r 11, restitution 0.5 | dynamic, mass 1, linearDamping ~0.6, continuousCollision | layer BALL, mask PLAYER, BALL_WALL, POST |
| Player | circle r 18, restitution 0.5 | dynamic, mass 2, linearDamping ~2.4, fixedRotation | layer PLAYER, mask PLAYER, BALL, PLAYER_WALL, POST |
| Pitch lines (4 segments, a gap at each goal) | boxes | static | BALL_WALL: stop only the ball |
| Outer border (screen edges) | boxes | static | PLAYER_WALL: players may leave the pitch, not the screen |
| Goal posts | circles r 8, restitution 0.5 | static | POST |
| Goal back and sides (the net) | boxes | static | BALL_WALL |
| Goal mouth | box, `sensor = true` | static | detects the ball (`rtype.TriggerEntered`) |

Values to tune in play: acceleration, kick strength, the damping. The numbers above come from haxball's defaults,
converted to per-second units.

## 4. Files

```
games/haxball/
  .luaurc, project.luau, README.md, PLAN.md
  scripts/shared/   main.luau (entry), rules.luau (constants, components, inputs), packets.luau

                    teams, movement, kick), ball.luau, match.luau (state machine, score, timer, kickoff reset)
  scripts/client/   main.luau (entry), state.luau, assets.luau, scene.luau (camera, pitch), menu.luau (title menu,

  assets/           sprites/, particles/, sounds/, fonts/ (Orbitron, copied from rtype_duel)
  tools/            make_art.py, make_sounds.py
```

**Plugins.** Server: `rtype.Physics`. Client: `rtype.Physics`, `rtype.Input`, `rtype.graphics`, `rtype.Shader`,
`rtype.Sprite`, `rtype.SpriteDraw`, `rtype.Shape`, `rtype.Text`, `rtype.Particle`, `rtype.audio`, `rtype.Ui`,
`rtype.PhysicsDebug` (F3). Gravity `{ 0, 0 }`.

## 5. Assets

Everything but the font is generated, so the game owns its assets and needs no download:

| Need | Made by |
|---|---|
| The pitch (grass stripes, lines, goals and nets), the players' discs, the ball, the kick ring, the marker | `tools/make_art.py` (Pillow) |
| Particle textures: dot, ring, smoke, star, confetti | `tools/make_art.py` |
| Kick, bounce, whistles, countdown beep, crowd cheer, UI click | `tools/make_sounds.py` (numpy), from sines and noise |
| Font | Orbitron (OFL), copied from `rtype_duel` |

## 6. Particles

| Effect | When | Look |
|---|---|---|
| `kick` | `game.Kicked` | a burst of 12 white sparks along the kick, plus a shock ring (`shape` circle, `edgeOnly`) |
| `ballTrail` | emitter on the ball, `rateScale` from `game.Ball.speed` | a soft white trail when the ball is fast, nothing at rest |
| `bounce` | `game.Bounce` | a few sparks off the post or the wall |
| `goal` | `game.Goal` | 150 confetti pieces in the scorer's team color, out of the goal, with gravity and spin |
| `dust` | emitter on each player, `rateScale` from its speed | small grass-green puffs while running |

## 7. Milestones

Each milestone ends playable: run `./aylo-runtime --server games/haxball` and two clients.

1. **Skeleton.** `project.luau`, the three entries, the pitch's colliders on the server, the pitch drawn with shapes on
   the client, the ball. F3 shows the colliders.
2. **Players.** Join with `game.Join` (sent directly for now with a default team), movement with acceleration and
   damping, pushing the ball, kicking. Server-to-client positions copied into the Transforms.
3. **Match.** Goal sensors, score, timer, kickoff reset and countdown, golden goal, end and restart. HUD texts.
4. **Menu.** Title screen with the name field and the team buttons; Escape reopens the picker; team switching and
   leaving (a disconnected player's entity is removed).
5. **Art.** Generate the sprites for the grass, players (facing their movement), ball (spinning with its
   speed), goals; names under players; local player marker.
6. **Juice.** Particles (section 6), sounds and crowd, banners, the kicking outline. Smooth the remote positions on the
   client if 60 snapshots per second look jittery (a short lerp toward the replicated position).
7. **Polish and ship.** Tune the physics in play, README (run, controls, credits of the assets), manual fixes below,
   the `verify` skill, commits with `commit-split`, a draft PR with `open-pr`.

## 8. The manual, and what may be missing

Found while planning:

- `manual/pages/networking.md` says sending your own messages is *planned*, but custom packets (`engine.net.packet`,
  `sendToServer`, `sendToClients`, `received`) are implemented (`docs/network/custom-packets.md`). Fix the page with the
  `write-manual` skill, since the game relies on it.
- `manual/pages/physics.md` does not list the fields of `rtype.Collider` (`radius`, `offset`, `sensor`, `friction`,
  `restitution`) and `rtype.RigidBody` (`type` values, `mass`, `linearDamping`, `angularDamping`). Add them.
- If the engine lacks something during the work (a quit call for the menu, interpolation of replicated positions,
  a font atlas from a TTF), do not touch C++: file an engine issue with the `plan-issues` skill and say so.
